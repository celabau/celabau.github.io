What We're Looking For

  • Strong programming/mathematics foundation (Python, deep learning frameworks, linear algebra/statistics)

Who Can Apply

PhD & Masters by Research candidates, as well as Honours students and short-term visiting students / research interns are all welcome to get in touch.

How to Apply

  1. Read the Research page and identify which pillar fits your interests.
