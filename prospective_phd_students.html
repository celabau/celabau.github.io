Message to Prospective PhD Students

1. General Information for Prospective PhD Students

Thank you for your interest in pursuing a PhD under my supervision. Please be aware that international PhD scholarships are highly competitive. Students admitted to my research group typically meet at least one of the following criteria:

  • Have an outstanding publication record, such as being the first or second author of papers published in IEEE/ACM Transactions journals, CORE A*/A conferences, or equivalent venues; or
  • Have graduated from a university ranked among the world's top 200, with a strong academic record (GPA of 80% or above).

2. Information for CSC Applicants from China

For CSC applicants from China, please contact me only if joining my team is your top priority. I consider applicants from top 100 Chinese universities. Please find the following message from Adelaide University's top-up scholarship offer:

The China Scholarship Council (CSC) – Adelaide University Partnership Scholarship Program are now open until 1 February 2027 for candidates intending to commence their PhD from July 2027. Partnership information and application instructions, including the Adelaide University nomination process, are available on the Research Scholarships webpage.

Adelaide University Research Scholarships

Kindly be aware that an application to Adelaide University and any subsequent nomination to the CSC does not guarantee success.

2027 Adelaide University Supplementary Scholarship (CSC) for Successful Applicants

  • CSC provides a stipend of about $22,800 p.a. for up to four years.
  • Adelaide University will provide a DVCRI-funded supplementary scholarship of $15,000 p.a. (indexed annually) for 3.5 years.
  • This brings the total stipend to the 2027 RTPS/AURS rate of $37,800 p.a.
  • No separate application is required from the student, supervisor, School, or College for the supplementary scholarship.

Please note, this arrangement is only valid for this CSC scholarship round and only for applicants nominated by the AUGRS to the CSC who commence in 2027.
